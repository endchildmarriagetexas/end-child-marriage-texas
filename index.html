About Us

The Texas Coalition to End Child Marriage advocates for policies, education, and survivor support initiatives that protect children from being forced or pressured into marriage before adulthood.


Our Mission

To educate Texans, support survivors, and advance laws that protect children from child marriage.


Our Vision

A Texas where every child has the opportunity to complete their education, pursue their dreams, and decide their future free from coercion.

Why It Matters

Education

Child marriage can interrupt schooling and limit future educational opportunities.

Economic Impact

Early marriage can contribute to long-term financial hardship and reduced earning potential.

Health & Safety

Young people in early marriages may face increased risks related to mental health, domestic violence, and isolation.

Human Rights

Children deserve the right to grow, learn, and make life-altering decisions as adults.

Survivor Voices

Tammi's Story

I was married in Lampasas, Texas, in May 2005, just one month after my 15th birthday.

Like many survivors of child marriage, I entered a life-changing legal and personal commitment before I had the opportunity to fully understand its consequences. While childhood should have been a time for education, personal growth, and planning for the future, marriage dramatically altered the course of my life.

For years, I carried the effects of that experience. Child marriage impacted my opportunities, my voice, and my ability to make decisions about my own future. As I learned more about child marriage laws in the United States, I was shocked to discover that I was not alone.

Since 2000, more than 41,000 minors have been married in Texas, and that number continues to grow every year. Behind every number is a child whose future may be altered by a decision made before reaching adulthood.

Rather than remaining silent, I chose to become an advocate.

Today, I work to raise awareness about the realities of child marriage, support survivors, educate communities, and advocate for stronger protections for children. I believe every child deserves the chance to grow up, complete their education, pursue their dreams, and make their own choices about marriage and adulthood.

This coalition was created to bring together survivors, advocates, parents, educators, faith leaders, and community members who believe that childhood should never be cut short by marriage.

Together, we can create a future where every child is protected, empowered, and free to choose their own path.

Featured Book

Have You Ever Worn a Scarlet Letter: Child Marriage in America, by Tammi Wooten, a Texas child marriage survivor, exposes one of the most overlooked human rights failures in the United States — the ongoing legality and devastating impact of child marriage. Through investigative research, survivor accounts, legal analysis, and historical context, this book reveals how loopholes in American law continue to allow minors to be married, often to adults, with lifelong consequences.
