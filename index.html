Every Child Deserves a Childhood

We are a coalition of survivors, advocates, parents, faith leaders, educators, and community members working to end child marriage in Texas and protect the rights and futures of all children.

Join the Coalition Take Action

About Us

The Texas Coalition to End Child Marriage advocates for policies, education, and survivor support initiatives that protect children from being forced or pressured into marriage before adulthood.


Our Mission

To educate Texans, support survivors, and advance laws that protect children from child marriage.


Our Vision

A Texas where every child has the opportunity to complete their education, pursue their dreams, and decide their future free from coercion.

Why It Matters

Education

Child marriage can interrupt schooling and limit future educational opportunities.

Economic Impact

Early marriage can contribute to long-term financial hardship and reduced earning potential.

Health & Safety

Young people in early marriages may face increased risks related to mental health, domestic violence, and isolation.

Human Rights

Children deserve the right to grow, learn, and make life-altering decisions as adults.

Survivor Voices

Tammi's Story

As a survivor of child marriage, I know firsthand how early marriage can affect education, opportunity, and personal freedom.

My experience inspired me to become an advocate for change and to help raise awareness about the impact child marriage continues to have on children across the United States.

Today, I share my story through advocacy, public education, and my book, Have You Ever Worn a Scarlet Letter?: Child Marriage in America, with the goal of helping protect future generations and supporting survivors.

Read My Story

Featured Book

Have You Ever Worn a Scarlet Letter: Child Marriage in America, by Tammi Wooten, a Texas child marriage survivor, exposes one of the most overlooked human rights failures in the United States — the ongoing legality and devastating impact of child marriage. Through investigative research, survivor accounts, legal analysis, and historical context, this book reveals how loopholes in American law continue to allow minors to be married, often to adults, with lifelong consequences. This book challenges lawmakers, parents, educators, and communities to confront a crisis hidden in plain sight — and to demand change. Buy on Amazon
